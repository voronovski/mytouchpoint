Frequently asked questions

Does My Touchpoint send greetings automatically?

No. My Touchpoint helps you plan greetings and reminds you when it is time to reach out. You review the message and choose when to send it using the system message or mail composer.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Check that notifications for My Touchpoint are allowed in your device’s Settings. Review your reminder schedule in My Touchpoint Settings, and check whether Focus or notification settings are silencing or delaying alerts.

How do I import contacts?

Use the contact import feature and allow access to the people you want to select. Only people you choose to import are saved in My Touchpoint. You can manage access in your device’s privacy settings or add people manually.

How do I check iCloud sync?

Open My Touchpoint Settings and check the Private iCloud backup section. Make sure your devices use the same Apple account, iCloud is available to the app, you have available iCloud storage, and your internet connection is working. Use “Sync iCloud now” to retry.

Why is message generation unavailable?

Generation requires a supported device, a compatible operating system, and Apple Intelligence enabled and available for your language and region. You can still write your own greetings and use templates.

How can I export or delete my information?

You can export a workspace archive from My Touchpoint Settings and edit or delete saved items in the app. Deleting the app does not automatically remove iCloud data, backups, or files you exported. See the retention and deletion section of our Privacy Policy for details.
